# NaClCon 2026 - Herding The Cats, Building Hacker Communities in the Early Days of the Internet


This presentation was given at [NaClCon](https://naclcon.com) in June of 2026

## Abstract

Have you ever wondered how hackers got anything done in the time before social
media? Join me as I delve into what it was like as a community organizer in the 90s and early
2000s. I will talk about the challenges, the big wins, and the bitter failures. Most importantly, I
will talk about what we lost along the way as we morphed into the community we are today.

## Speaker Bio
